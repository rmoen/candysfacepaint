Meet the Artist

Hi, I'm Candy!

Art is dear to my heart, and face painting is how I share that love with the world.

I'm Candace — but everyone calls me Candy! I'm a professional face painter based in Oregon City, bringing color and joy to faces all across the Portland metro area and beyond.

Every brushstroke is a chance to spark joy. Whether it's a child's eyes lighting up when they see themselves transformed into a butterfly, tiger, or superhero, or an adult letting their inner kid shine at a festival — those moments are everything to me.

I use only professional-grade, skin-safe paints and take pride in creating designs that are vibrant, detailed, and made to last through the fun. From delicate princess crowns to fierce dragon faces, I love bringing imagination to life!

Good to Know

Sweet & Simple FAQs

What areas do you serve?

I'm based in Oregon City and serve the entire Portland metro area — Portland, Lake Oswego, West Linn, Milwaukie, Gladstone, Beaverton, Tigard, Gresham, Happy Valley, Hillsboro, Tualatin, and Clackamas. Happy to travel further for the right event — just ask!

Are the paints safe for kids and sensitive skin?

Yes! I use only professional-grade, FDA-compliant, skin-safe face paints. Designs are suitable for all ages, from toddlers to adults.

What types of events do you do?

Birthday parties, festivals and fairs, corporate events, school events (carnivals, field days, fundraisers), holiday and themed events, weddings, baby showers, and private parties of all kinds.

What do I need to provide?

I bring all supplies — paints, brushes, glitter, and design boards. You just need to provide a seat for guests and a spot for me to set up.

How far in advance should I book?

Book as early as you can — weekends fill up fast, especially in summer festival season! Reach out through the booking form, or call or text (503) 560-5455.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the type of event, guest count, and duration. Fill out the booking form below for a free, no-obligation quote — I'll get back to you within 24 hours.
