Meet the Artist

Hi, I'm Candy! 💜

Art is dear to my heart, and face painting is how I share that love with the world.

I'm Candace — but everyone calls me Candy! I'm a professional face painter based in Portland, Oregon, bringing color and joy to faces all across the metro area and beyond.

Every brushstroke is a chance to spark joy. Whether it's a child's eyes lighting up when they see themselves transformed into a butterfly, tiger, or superhero, or an adult letting their inner kid shine at a festival — those moments are everything to me.

I use only professional-grade, skin-safe paints and take pride in creating designs that are vibrant, detailed, and made to last through the fun. From delicate princess crowns to fierce dragon faces, I love bringing imagination to life!
